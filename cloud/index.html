How it will work

No terminal. No server. Just the idea.

  • 01

    Connect your plan and your code

    Sign in with your Claude or ChatGPT plan, or add an API key. Connect GitHub, or start fresh and we'll create the repo for you.

  • 02

    Describe the idea

    Scout asks a few questions to frame it. Agents look at what exists, write the plan and turn it into a trail of decisions and work.

  • 03

    Answer from anywhere

    Agents work in their own cloud workspaces and open pull requests. You get the questions that matter, and a trip report of what changed.

Two ways to run Teamtrail

Same Scout. Your machine, or ours.

Both run the same engine, and your trails move between them as plain files.

Yours, all the way

Your plan, your code, your call.

  • Your own model plan

    Agents use your Claude or ChatGPT plan, or your API key. You sign in with Anthropic or OpenAI directly; we never handle your credentials or resell model usage.

  • Isolated workspaces

    Every user gets their own workspace. Nothing is shared between accounts.

  • Nothing merges without you

    Agents open pull requests. What ships is always your decision.

  • Take it home anytime

    Export your trails as plain files and keep going with Teamtrail Local, for free.
