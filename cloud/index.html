In early access

What you get today.

Teamtrail Local is built for one person. Cloud adds what matters once the Lookout has to be reachable, and once there are more of you.

  • Your agents, running while you're away

    Turn on Run my agents for a trail and Cloud runs Claude Code or Codex on its ready work, two of yours at a time, each in a sandbox of its own. It claims a ticket, works it, asks you when a call needs you, and stops when nothing is left.

  • Your repositories, through our GitHub App

    An owner connects GitHub with the Teamtrail GitHub App and picks which repositories agents may work in. Each run gets a short-lived token for its one repository.

  • On your own keys

    Agents use your own Anthropic or OpenAI API key, or one key an owner sets for the whole workspace. The provider bills whoever the key belongs to; we never resell model usage.

  • Verified, on the commit they pushed

    When an agent says a ticket is done, Cloud runs your approved checks on the exact commit it pushed, in a fresh sandbox of their own. The ticket's Verified by Teamtrail proof names that commit, so a review starts from evidence, not from the agent's word.

  • Nothing merges without you

    Each ticket lands on its own branch, and Cloud opens a pull request for it in your repository. What ships is always your decision. Every run's log, and your agent hours this month, are on the Agents page.

  • A workspace of its own

    Each workspace runs its own engine at its own address, with its own storage, backed up daily and weekly. Nothing is shared between workspaces.

  • People and roles

    Everyone signs in with GitHub; you invite them by their login. Owners change settings, members answer and approve, viewers read. A removal takes effect on the next click.

  • High-risk calls, delegated

    Owners always make high-risk calls and can name others who may. Everyone else sees those questions but can't answer them.

  • Take it home anytime

    Owners download every trail, ticket and decision as a zip of plain files and can carry on with Teamtrail Local, for free. Deleting a workspace deletes its data for good.
