What holds the trail

High-risk questions, verified work ready to merge, a plan to approve, reroutes and setting requests, checks failing on main, agents with nothing left to do.

What can wait

Questions agents go on without, criteria and knowledge to review, guides to approve, and verified work that came back. One digest, at a time you pick.

What agents are doing

Progress and activity never notify. They're in the app when you open it.

On the go

Answer, approve and merge from your phone.

Every workspace in one place: trails by workspace, their progress, the agents at work and what happened lately. The map stays in the Lookout.

Answers that count

Answer any question, high-risk ones too. It counts as your answer in the Lookout would, and the Logbook records it as yours.

Merge with its proof
