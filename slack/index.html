Scout in Slack

Answer your agents from Slack.

Scout is Teamtrail's Slack app. Your agents' questions, finished work to approve and trail updates reach you in Slack, and your answers go straight back to the Teamtrail on your machine. Free, no account.

What Scout does

The calls that need you, where you already are.

Only what crosses your interrupt threshold gets its own message. Everything else waits in one digest per trail.

Questions

Answer agents in a message

An urgent question arrives with answer buttons, or Reply to answer in your own words. The waiting agent gets it right away.

Approvals

Sign off finished work

Verified work that waits for you comes with Approve and Rework. Agents' requests to change a trail's settings come too, to approve in the Lookout or decline in Slack.

Digests

One message per trail

Everything that doesn't interrupt goes into a digest at your team's pace, instead of a stream of notifications.

/scout

Ask from any channel

/scout status for a trail at a glance, /scout inbox for what waits on you, /scout decide to record a decision.

Save as decision

Keep what a thread decided

A message shortcut that records a Slack thread as a decision on a trail, or settles the ticket it was about.

Home tab

See where you stand

Scout's Home tab shows whether it's connected for you, with a link to your Lookout.

Connect in a minute

One app, added from the Lookout.

  • 01

    Open Settings → Slack

    In the Lookout on your machine, or start it there with npx teamtrail connect slack. Choose Add Scout to Slack.

  • 02

    Allow it in Slack

    Slack's own screen asks you to add Scout to your workspace. A workspace admin may have to approve the app once.

  • 03

    Confirm it's you

    Back in the Lookout, check "Connect as @you in your workspace?" and choose Connect. That Slack account is the owner; nothing is saved before you confirm.

Your trails stay home

What passes through, and what never does.

Slack can't reach your laptop, so Scout works through a small Teamtrail service. Your Teamtrail keeps one outbound connection to it.

Passes through the Scout service

In memory, on its way between Slack and your machine. Never stored or logged.

  • Questions, answers and digests that Scout posts or you send
  • Your clicks, /scout commands and Save as decision threads

Never leaves your machine

Your Teamtrail decides; the service only carries messages.

  • Your trails, tickets and code
  • Decisions, approvals and the evidence behind them
  • The gates that decide what counts as your word

The service keeps only what it needs to deliver: your workspace's Slack token, encrypted, and a link between your Slack account and your Teamtrail, stored as a hash. See the privacy policy.

Still your call

The riskiest calls stay in the Lookout.

  • High risk needs the Lookout

    A question or a finished change that your risk policy rates high shows Open in Lookout in Slack. An answer given in Slack never counts for it.

  • Settings change in the Lookout

    Skipping discovery, or changing a trail's mode or destination, is approved in the Lookout. You can decline it in Slack.

  • Only the people you choose

    Buttons count only for the owner and the people they allow, and only on messages your own Teamtrail posted.

  • Off means off

    When Teamtrail isn't running, Scout says so and does nothing. Nothing waits on a server to act for you later.
