You decide what matters

Only the turns that need you.

Agents ask, you decide, and the decision is written down.

  • One list, most important first

    Each item says why it ranks there. Only urgent ones interrupt you.

  • Answer in a click

    Beside the ticket on the map, in Slack, or with /scout inbox.

  • Risky calls are yours by rule

    Data, logins, billing: agents can't make those calls themselves.

  • Paced to your review

    New work waits when reviews pile up; code nobody asked for is flagged. Review capacity

  • While you were away

    Scout tells you what changed, and what it cost.

Verified, not claimed

“Done” is a claim. Scout checks it.

You agree on done before work starts. Every gate can run in observe first, then hold work for you. How verification works.

  • Checks the work can't change

    A pass counts only if the work didn't edit what the check runs or weaken the tests.

  • The whole trail, verified together

    Once its pull requests merge, the checks run again on main, all tickets together.

  • Reviews by a different model

    The proof shows the author's and the reviewer's models.

  • Rules from past mistakes

    Touch these files, run this check or answer this question, with the incident behind it.

  • Flaky checks caught

    Failed, then passed on the same commit? Not verified until you say so.

  • A signed proof on every pull request

    Checks, review and decisions, signed and checkable by anyone, as a required check.

On the record

Every call and every result, kept.

  • The logbook

    The question, your words, the alternatives and who decided, by name.

  • You don't get asked twice

    An agent gets your earlier decision back instead of asking again.

  • A scorecard for your agents

    Verified first time, rework and cost, by model and agent, from your own tickets.

  • What came back

    Work verified and later reopened or reverted, counted, with a rule to add from it. Trail stats

Local-first and safe

On your machine, by your rules.

What only you approve, and what it protects against.

  • Plain files or your tracker

    Markdown in your repo, or GitHub Issues and Linear, synced both ways.

  • Approvals live with you

    Editing files never opens a gate: changes wait for you on your machine.

  • Paired browsers only

    The Lookout opens only where you signed in. A phone pairs with a code.

  • Never on your behalf

    It never answers, approves or starts agents for you.
